# Claims status

One reconciled list of the claims made in `REPORT.md` and in the follow-ups, as of 13/10/2026, after the calibration, FLEURS, TTS-error, phantom (v1 and v2) and literature-check follow-ups. Nothing here is new analysis: every number is read from the file named next to it, and a skeptic pass re-derived the key ones from the CSVs (section E). `REPORT.md` now carries a dated update box and marked corrections that follow this table (section D).

**Status.**
- **holds**: still true as worded, possibly with a narrower scope noted in the evidence.
- **weakened**: the direction stands, but the wording overstates what the data show.
- **replaced**: the claim as worded is wrong or misleading; the replacement wording is given.
- **new**: a result of a follow-up that the original `REPORT.md` did not contain.

**Prior work** (reference numbers from `RELATED-WORK.md`):
- **replicates**: the result is already published; we confirm it in this setting.
- **applies the method of**: we use a published method; the result itself is ours.
- **consistent with**: a published result points the same way, on a different question or setup.
- **extends**: the question is published, and we add a factor, inference or a domain.
- **new**: no published precedent found by the literature review (which ran out of search budget for 2025 to 2026; see its last section).

CIs are 95 % unless stated. "Points" are WER percentage points. LibriSpeech CIs are speaker-cluster bootstraps; FLEURS CIs are sentence bootstraps (no speaker ids) and are probably too narrow. Phantom rates count a clip as a phantom when its transcript is non-empty after `asr.normalize` (the scoring normalizer).
